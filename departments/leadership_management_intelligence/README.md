# Department 021: Leadership & Management Intelligence (`leadership_management_intelligence`)

## Overview
The **Leadership & Management Intelligence Department** delivers an enterprise multi-agent pipeline designed to measure team size capacity, analyze leadership styles, score conflict resolution skills, evaluate strategic vision clarity, audit cross-functional influence, track team retention rates, and generate executive coaching growth plans.

---

## Internal 10-Agent Architecture

### Deterministic Agents (7)
1. **TeamSizeCapacityMeterAgent**: Measures managed team size and direct report span of control.
2. **LeadershipStyleAnalyzerAgent**: Analyzes delegation and leadership traits.
3. **ConflictResolutionScorerAgent**: Evaluates de-escalation tactics and conflict resolution.
4. **StrategicVisionScorerAgent**: Scores vision clarity and OKR goal alignment rates.
5. **CrossFunctionalInfluenceAgent**: Measures stakeholder influence across partner teams.
6. **RetentionPerformanceAuditorAgent**: Audits team retention and voluntary attrition.
7. **LeadershipScorerAgent**: Master deterministic aggregator for leadership metrics.

### Reasoning Agents (2)
8. **StrategicLeadershipNarrativeAgent**: Formulates executive leadership management summaries.
9. **ExecutiveCoachingPlannerAgent**: Generates executive coaching goals and action items.

### Orchestrator Agent (1)
10. **LeadershipManagementOrchestratorAgent**: Master Orchestrator Agent uniting leadership metrics and executive coaching plans.
